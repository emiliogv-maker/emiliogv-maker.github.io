Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 3/4 cup unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons oil (any kind)
  • 1 and 1/3 cups granulated sugar (divided)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 3/4 cup chocolate chips

Optional Enhancements

Add these ingredients to make your brownies even better:

  • 1/2 cup chopped nuts (walnuts or pecans)
  • 1/2 teaspoon espresso powder to enhance chocolate flavor
  • 1/4 cup chocolate chunks for extra gooey texture
