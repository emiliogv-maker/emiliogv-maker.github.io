Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a 9×9-inch baking pan with parchment paper and spray lightly.
  2. Sift together flour, baking powder, salt, cocoa powder, and espresso powder.
  3. In a medium saucepan, melt butter, oil, and 1/3 cup sugar over medium heat.
  4. Whisk eggs, yolk, vanilla, and remaining sugar in a large bowl.
  5. Gradually mix in warm butter mixture.
  6. Add dry ingredients and chocolate chips. Do not overmix.
  7. Pour batter into pan and smooth top.
  8. Bake 28–30 minutes until edges firm and top slightly cracked.
  9. Cool completely before slicing.

Pro Tips

  • Oil & Butter: This combination keeps this recipe moist and flavorful. Butter gives it more flavor and oil it more moisture. That’s why we use both.
  • Don’t over mix: Stop mixing the brownie batter when you still see the slightest trace of dry ingredients. This guarantees you don’t over mix. Over mixing leads to dry brownies..
  • Avoid Over Baking: Take them out of the oven when they still have wiggle in the middle. Bake times are just estimates. So although its recommended to bake for 27 to 30 minutes, this could vary depending on your oven and pan.

Conclusion

Good brownies don't need extravagant ingredients; technique is key. With just the basics—quality chocolate, butter, sugar, eggs, and flour—you can create something truly indulgent if you treat each step with care. Properly melting the chocolate, mixing the batter just enough to keep it dense and fudgy, and pulling the pan from the oven at the perfect moment all matter far more than any fancy additions. When you focus on the fundamentals, those simple ingredients transform into brownies with a rich, intense flavor and the ideal balance of crackly top and soft center.
